# Snip Design Tokens

## Direction

Dark, minimal link workspace with a centered hero, a warm full-width glow, quiet borders, and generous breathing room. Borrow visual cues only; use no third-party logo, naming, or copy.

## Tokens

- Background: `#0B0B0E`; surfaces: `#151518` and `#1D1B1E`.
- Text: `#F7F2EF`; muted: `#A59DA0`; faint: `#777176`.
- Accent: coral `#FF765F`, orange `#FFB36B`, pink `#E97B9A`; gradient `linear-gradient(110deg, #FF765F 0%, #FFB36B 52%, #E97B9A 100%)`.
- Glow: low-opacity coral and peach radial light over a full-width horizontal tint; fixed to the viewport top, blurred, and pointer-transparent.
- Type: `Manrope, Segoe UI Variable, Aptos, sans-serif`; monospace labels use `SFMono-Regular, Consolas, monospace`. Scale: hero 60/1.04 (40px mobile), section 18px, body 14-16px, labels 10-11px.
- Spacing: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64px.
- Radii: chat control pill `999px`; small controls `12px`; cards `20px`.
- Borders: `1px solid rgba(255, 247, 242, 0.11)`. Cards use `0 24px 70px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.32)`; focus rings use a faint coral/orange halo.

## Snip Mapping

- Header and title: compact brand/status bar above a centered, bold hero and muted subline.
- URL form: centered chat-style pill; transparent input and attached gradient action.
- Result and error: separate rounded surface notices with warm success and coral error accents.
- Links table: raised dark card with subtle row dividers, muted destinations, and orange short-code links.
